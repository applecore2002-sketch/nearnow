NearNow

Open places, on tap for AI assistants.

NearNow is an MCP connector that lets AI assistants — Meta's Muse, xAI's Grok, and any MCP-compatible agent — answer “where is the nearest open pharmacy / supermarket / EV charger?” with real, attributed data.

What assistants can ask

💊 Pharmaciesdrugstores and 24-hour options
🛒 Supermarketsgrocery stores with hours
⛽ Gas stationsfuel, mostly 24/7 in the US
🔌 EV chargingcharging points and operators
📚 Librariespublic library branches
📮 Post officespostal service locations

Honest opening hours — never guessed

Every result carries a confidence label, so an assistant can tell its user exactly how sure it is.

confirmed — hours come from the place's OpenStreetMap record.
estimated — chain-standard or category-typical hours (e.g. “Costco: Mon–Fri 10:00–20:30”).
unknown — no data. NearNow says so instead of inventing an answer, and never reports a place as “open now” without basis.

Built for assistants, not browsers

NearNow speaks the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Connectors point their agent at our endpoint and get three tools:

search_places      → "pharmacies near Pasadena, CA open now"
get_place_details  → hours, address, phone, exact location
list_categories    → what this connector can answer

Own a place? You're already in.

Venue data comes from OpenStreetMap, the free, community-built map. If your business is missing or its hours are wrong, you can fix it yourself in minutes at openstreetmap.org/edit — and every AI assistant using NearNow (and much of the open web) sees the correction. No listing fees, ever.
