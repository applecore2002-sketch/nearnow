1. What NearNow is

NearNow is a software connector (an MCP server) that lets AI assistants search public-places information — locations, categories, and opening hours — derived from OpenStreetMap, the free community-built world map. NearNow is free to use.

Place data comes from OpenStreetMap and from general chain-standard schedules, and may be incomplete or out of date. Every result includes a confidence label (confirmed / estimated / unknown) describing how reliable the opening hours are. Always verify critical information (especially hours, especially late at night or on holidays) directly with the place before traveling. NearNow does not guarantee that any place is open, exists, or suits your needs.
